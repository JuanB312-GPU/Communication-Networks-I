About Universidad Distrital Francisco José de Caldas

Universidad Distrital Francisco José de Caldas is a public higher education institution located in Bogotá, Colombia. Founded in 1948, this university has established itself as a prominent academic center, offering undergraduate and graduate programs in various fields of knowledge. Its focus on research and technological development makes it a reference in the country's educational landscape.

The university is committed to the holistic formation of its students, promoting not only technical knowledge but also the development of social and ethical skills to contribute to the progress of Colombian society.

With an emphasis on innovation and problem-solving, Universidad Distrital is a space for creativity and collaborative learning, shaping professionals capable of tackling the challenges of the modern world.
